Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Lignio Private Limited collects through thelignio.com and how we use it.

1. What we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, delivery address, PIN code, the products you order and any note you add.
  • Messages: what you send us on WhatsApp, email or phone.
  • Payment details: handled by our payment provider (such as Razorpay). We receive only the payment status and reference, never your card or UPI credentials.
  • Technical data: basic data your browser shares, such as device type and pages visited, collected by our hosting provider for security and performance.

2. How we use it

  • To confirm, pack, ship and deliver your order
  • To contact you about your order, delivery or refund
  • To keep records required by law, such as tax and food-safety records
  • To improve our products and website

We do not sell your personal information. We send you offers only if you have asked to receive them.

3. Who we share it with

  • Courier and delivery partners, so they can deliver your order
  • Payment providers, to process payments and refunds
  • Our website hosting provider (Netlify), which stores order form submissions
  • Government authorities, when required by law

4. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal and tax requirements, and then delete them.

5. Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop sending you messages. Contact us using the details below.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including HTTPS on our website. No method of sending data over the internet is fully secure.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

8. Grievance Officer

For any privacy concern or complaint, contact our Grievance Officer. We will acknowledge your complaint within 48 hours and resolve it within 30 days.
