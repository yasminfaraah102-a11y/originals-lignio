How to make a perfect cup

Brew it like home

For one cup of milk tea. Use a little more tea for a stronger cup.

1

Boil water

Bring 180 ml of water to a boil in a pan.

2

Add tea

Add 1 teaspoon (2 to 2.5 g) of your blend.

3

Simmer

Let it boil for 2 to 3 minutes to open up the spices.

4

Finish

Add milk and sugar to taste. Strain and enjoy.
