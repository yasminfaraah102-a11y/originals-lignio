Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Where we deliver

We currently deliver across India. If we cannot reach your PIN code, we will tell you before you pay.

2. Dispatch time

Orders are packed and dispatched within 1 to 3 working days after your order and payment are confirmed. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are dispatched the next working day.

3. Delivery time

  • Kerala and Assam: usually 2 to 5 working days after dispatch
  • Rest of India: usually 4 to 8 working days after dispatch
  • Remote areas, including parts of the North East and islands: may take longer

These are estimates. Courier delays, weather, strikes or holidays can sometimes extend delivery times.

4. Delivery charges

Delivery is charged once per order, based on your PIN code:

Kerala and Assam: ₹50
Other North East states and Sikkim: ₹100
Rest of India: ₹80
Orders of ₹899 and above: FREE delivery anywhere in India

The delivery charge is shown on the order page before you place your order.

5. Tracking

Once your order ships, we share the courier name and tracking number on WhatsApp or email.

6. Failed delivery

Please give a complete address and a phone number you will answer. If a parcel comes back because the address was wrong or the receiver was unavailable, we may charge shipping again to resend it.

7. Damaged parcels

If the outer box looks damaged or opened, please note it with the courier and send us a photo within 48 hours. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
