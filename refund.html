Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Because our teas are food products, we cannot take back opened or used packs for hygiene and safety reasons. We will always make it right if something goes wrong with your order.

1. Cancellations

  • Orders cannot be cancelled once they are confirmed and paid.
  • If we are unable to fulfil your order, for example because a product is out of stock or we cannot deliver to your area, we will cancel it and give you a full refund.

2. Damaged, wrong or missing items

If your pack arrives damaged, tampered with, leaking, past its best-before date, or is the wrong product:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Send a clear photo or short video of the pack and the outer box.
  • Keep the product and packaging until we resolve it.

After checking, we will send a free replacement or give a full refund, as you prefer.

3. What we cannot refund

  • Opened or partly used packs, unless the product was faulty
  • Change of mind or taste preference after delivery
  • Delays or failed delivery caused by a wrong address or an unreachable phone number

4. How refunds are paid

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card, net banking or wallet).
  • We start the refund within 2 working days of approving it. Your bank or payment provider usually credits it within 5 to 7 working days.
  • For cash-on-delivery orders, we refund to a UPI ID or bank account you give us.
